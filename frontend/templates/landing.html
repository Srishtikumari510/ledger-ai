FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents can the platform process?

Invoices, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss statements, and Cash Flow Statements — as native or scanned PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 3 pages per document.

How accurate is the AI extraction?

Extraction combines OCR and an LLM with a strict structured-output schema. Values are traceable to source text and page numbers, and validation catches any inconsistencies before they matter.

How does validation work?

Every document type has financial relationships (e.g. subtotal + tax − discount ≈ total). Checks return formula, operands, calculated value, reported value, variance, and PASS / FAIL / NOT_APPLICABLE status.

Can the platform integrate with existing systems?

Yes. Everything is exposed through a consistent REST API: multipart upload, GET by document name, list, and health endpoints. Responses are predictable JSON.

Is there a REST API?

Yes — documented via Swagger / OpenAPI at /docs. Four core endpoints plus health: process, get-by-name, list, and health check.

How is sensitive data protected?

Secrets are read from environment variables and never committed. Uploads are validated, files are handled safely, and errors are sanitized to avoid leaking internal details.
